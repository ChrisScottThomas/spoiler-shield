# Comments hidden everywhere, with a Safe List and no master switch

Comments are hidden on every Watch Page by default. The only ways to see them are a per-video Reveal, which lasts for that one loaded video, or (from v2) adding the channel to the Safe List. We deliberately have no master on/off switch and no "hide only on these channels" mode. A switch is easy to forget to turn back on, and the next replay is then spoiled. A block list only protects channels you've already been spoiled on. Revealing comments on a video you didn't need protecting costs one click; a spoiler can't be undone.

## Considered Options

- **Master on/off switch** on the toolbar icon: rejected, because of the forgotten-switch spoiler above.
- **Block list** ("hide only on these channels"), alone or as a selectable mode: rejected for v1 and v2. It doubles the settings and the testing, and it gets the safety default backwards.
